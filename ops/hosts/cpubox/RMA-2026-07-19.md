## Submitted diagnosis

- BMC SEL contains 43 asserted uncorrectable ECC events on CPU2 channel I:
  - 35 decoded as `DIMMI1(CPU2)`, raw event data `a19a81`.
  - 8 decoded as `DIMMI2(CPU2)`, raw event data `a19b81`.
- SMBIOS maps the affected physical module to `P2-DIMMI1`, a Samsung
  `M321R4GA3EB2-CCPPF` 32 GiB DDR5 ECC RDIMM, serial `80CE012515017B8A0E`.
- Unexpected crashes and reboots correlate with the recent fatal ECC sequence. OS EDAC/rasdaemon
  counters do not disprove the fault because they reset at boot; the BMC SEL persists across resets.
- The most likely fault is the DIMM. Residual suspects are the CPU2 channel-I slot/traces/socket or
  CPU2 integrated memory controller.

The complete local fault analysis and safety notes remain in

## Requested warranty action

1. Advance cross-ship one validated `MEM-DR532L-SL02-ER64` or vendor-approved equivalent under
   first-year coverage.
2. Coordinate onsite installation at the Haymarket site, or provide an onsite-service entitlement
   decision or quote if field labor is not included.
3. Preserve the SEL until exported; then replace or swap `P2-DIMMI1`, clear the SEL, and run
   sustained memory validation.
4. If uncorrectable ECC remains on CPU2 channel I with known-good memory, keep the case open and
   escalate to CPU2/socket/H13DSH motherboard diagnosis.
5. Coordinate all power-down activity. Do not reimage the host or alter storage or data.

Onsite dispatch is a request, not a confirmed entitlement. Supermicro documents onsite service as
a separate SLA offering. Standard RMA processing also requires an assigned RMA number before any
part is returned.
